SHMUP is the 3D shoot'em up Fabien Sanglard wrote in 2009, in the tradition of the Japanese arcade shooters. Reborn brings it back to today's iPhones and iPads: full screen, Metal rendering, Game Center, and multiplayer for up to four ships.

Five acts, and the old pleasure of a shoot'em up that asks something of you. You go in, you die, you go back in, and one evening your hands know the way through. No open world, no cutscene to sit through, every act open from the first launch: a ship, a screen filling with bullets, and the one line through it you have to find.

Nothing is random. Every wave, every bullet and every boss move is deterministic, so the same run plays the same way twice. Learn the patterns, then beat them.

PLAY TOGETHER
- Local network: up to four players on the same Wi-Fi, no account needed.
- Online: matches through Game Center, two to four ships.
- A shared pool of lives and a team score. A fallen ship comes back at the next act.

YOUR SHIP
- Four ships (Falcon, Viper, Phoenix and Ghost) and four bullet colours, one of them Invisible, for those who want to fly blind.
- One finger to fly and fire, a double tap for the special shot, and a tutorial that shows it all. Sound and music in Others > Options.
- A pause button during every act.
- Your best scores on a Game Center leaderboard.

OPEN SOURCE
SHMUP is free software under the GNU GPLv3. The original engine and this modernization are both on GitHub. No ads, no purchases, no tracking, nothing collected: your score goes to Game Center, and during a multiplayer match your ship's moves go to the other players' devices.
