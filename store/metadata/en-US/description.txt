SHMUP is the 3D shoot'em up Fabien Sanglard wrote in 2009, inspired by Treasure's Ikaruga. Reborn brings it back to today's iPhones and iPads: full screen, Metal rendering, Game Center, and multiplayer for up to four ships.

Fly through five acts on rails. Dawn, Hope, Dusk, Rain and the Final act each have their own city, their own waves and their own tricks: a side-view beat in the middle of Dusk, a storm to cross in Rain, and at the end a boss with two living, skeletal arms that reach for you like a pincer. Tear an arm off and it fights on with the other.

Nothing is random. Every wave, every bullet and every boss move is deterministic, so the same run plays the same way twice. Learn the patterns, then beat them.

PLAY TOGETHER
- Local network: up to four players on the same Wi-Fi, no account needed.
- Online: matches through Game Center, two to four ships.
- A shared pool of lives and a team score. A fallen ship comes back at the next act.

YOUR SHIP
- Three ships to choose from and four bullet colours.
- Two control schemes, swipe or virtual pad, each with its own tutorial.
- Your best scores on a Game Center leaderboard.

OPEN SOURCE
SHMUP is free software under the GNU GPLv3. The original engine and this modernization are both on GitHub. No ads, no purchases, no tracking, nothing collected: the only data that leaves your device is the score you send to Game Center.
