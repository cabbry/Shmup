First App Store release of SHMUP Reborn.

- Five acts: Dawn, Hope, Dusk, Rain and the Final act.
- The final boss has skeletal arms: they breathe, they recoil, they pinch. Tear one off.
- Local network and Game Center multiplayer, two to four ships.
- Three ships, four bullet colours, two control schemes with tutorials.
- Game Center leaderboard.
- English and French.
