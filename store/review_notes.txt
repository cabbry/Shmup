SHMUP Reborn is a free, open-source (GNU GPLv3) modernization of SHMUP, the 3D shoot'em up Fabien Sanglard wrote and published on the App Store in 2009 (https://github.com/fabiensanglard/Shmup). He agreed to this release. Our source: https://github.com/cabbry/Shmup.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE 5.6 REJECTIONS OF 5.0.11 AND 5.0.12
We went through the whole app for anything a reviewer could not see, and removed it:
- The switches our simulator test scripts used (invulnerability, autofire, scene jump, forced menu) and their diagnostic output are no longer compiled into App Store builds. A test level only those scripts could open is no longer in the app.
- The page in the iOS Settings app is gone. It held three player options, a developer statistics overlay, a Game Center switch that did nothing, and six 2009 renderer settings hidden by misspelt row types. Controls, sound and music are now in the game: Others > Options.
- Code for a 2010 "Lite" trial, an unused replay/upload screen and a button placed off-screen are removed.
- All five acts are open from the first launch; they used to unlock one by one.
- A visible pause button replaces an undocumented five-finger gesture.
Nothing in the app is hidden, remotely configured, or date- or region-dependent. Our release pipeline now checks every archive for test switches, diagnostic output, test data and hidden settings before it is uploaded.

HOW TO REACH EVERYTHING (no account needed)
Home: Game Solo / Game Multi / Others.
- Game Solo > Easy / Normal / Insane (6 / 3 / 1 lives) > any act: Dawn, Hope, Dusk, Rain, Final (the boss with arms).
- In an act: one finger moves the ship and fires while it touches the screen; a double tap launches the special shot. The "II" next to the score pauses the game: Resume / Quit.
- Others > Options: controls (swipe or virtual pad), sound, music. With the pad: move at bottom left, fire at bottom right.
- Others > Custom: 4 ships (Falcon, Viper, Phoenix, Ghost; Ghost is the Falcon hull drawn translucent) and 4 bullet colours (Red, Blue, Invisible, Yellow; Invisible does not draw your shots, a challenge option).
- Others > Tutorial: a short tutorial for the control scheme chosen in Options. Others > Demo: act Dawn playing itself. Others > Scores: the Game Center leaderboard. Others > Credits.
- Game Multi > Local (same Wi-Fi; asks for Local Network access; the Bonjour service is named _DodgeServer, the game's 2009 code name) or Online (Game Center) > 2 / 3 / 4 Players > act. Lives are shared (3 per player) and the score is a team score; a fallen ship returns at the next act. Enemies are tougher with more ships, and act Rain adds one extra wave for parties of three or more. A single device reaches the waiting screen; a match needs the other devices. The first iPhone screenshot was taken during a two-device match.

GAME CENTER is offered at launch. If it is declined, Scores and Online show where to sign in (iOS Settings > Game Center).

No purchases, ads, user-generated content, chat, web views or analytics. Network traffic: Game Center, and game data between the players' devices during a match.
