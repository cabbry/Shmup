First App Store release of SHMUP Reborn.

- Five acts, all open from the first launch: Dawn, Hope, Dusk, Rain and the Final act.
- The final boss has skeletal arms: they breathe, they recoil, they pinch. Tear one off.
- Local network and Game Center multiplayer, two to four ships.
- Four ships, four bullet colours, one-finger controls with a tutorial, an Options screen.
- A pause button during every act.
- Game Center leaderboard.
- Menus in English and French.
