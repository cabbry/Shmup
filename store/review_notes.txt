SHMUP Reborn is a free, open-source (GNU GPLv3) modernization of SHMUP, the 3D shoot'em up Fabien Sanglard wrote and published on the App Store in 2009 (https://github.com/fabiensanglard/Shmup). He approved this release by e-mail on 23 September 2026. Our source: https://github.com/cabbry/Shmup.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE 5.6 REJECTIONS OF 5.0.11 AND 5.0.12
We went through the whole app for anything a reviewer could not see, and removed it:
- Twelve switches used only by our simulator test scripts (invulnerability, autofire, extra ships in solo, act jump and replay, forced menu, boss-arm test modes, offline rail baking, a log file) and their diagnostic output are no longer compiled into App Store builds. A test level only they could open is gone.
- The page in the iOS Settings app is gone (three player options, a developer statistics overlay, a Game Center switch that did nothing, six 2009 renderer settings hidden by misspelt row types).
- Removed: a 2010 "Lite" trial, the 2009 virtual-pad controls, an unused replay screen, a button placed off-screen, 2010 developer tools (time jump, input recorder, frame grabber), game files nothing loaded.
- All five acts are open from the first launch; they used to unlock one by one.
- A five-finger gesture went back to the menu; only the tutorial mentioned it. A visible pause button replaces it; the tutorial points to its [ BACK ] button.
Nothing is hidden, remotely configured, or date- or region-dependent; the menus follow the device language (English or French; the tutorial, HUD and end card are English). Every build is checked for all of this before upload.

HOW TO REACH EVERYTHING (no app account; Scores and Online use the device's Game Center sign-in)
Home: Game Solo / Game Multi / Others (French: Jeu Solo / Jeu Multi / Autres).
- Game Solo > Easy / Normal / Insane (6 / 3 / 1 lives; the HUD counts spare ships, x5 / x2 / x0) > any act: Dawn, Hope, Dusk, Rain, Final (the boss with arms).
- In an act: one finger moves the ship and fires while it touches the screen; a double tap launches the special shot (every five seconds). "[II]" next to the score pauses: Resume / Quit (in a match the others play on). Leaving the app pauses a solo game (back with a 3-2-1 count) and leaves a match.
- Others > Options: sound, music.
- Others > Custom: 4 ships (Ghost is the Falcon hull, translucent) and 4 bullet colours (Red, Blue, Yellow, and Invisible: your shots are not drawn, a challenge).
- Others > Tutorial: a one-minute walkthrough of the controls that plays itself. Others > Demo: act Dawn playing itself. Both have [ BACK ] at the top (the Demo from 8 s, once the ship flies). Others > Scores: the Game Center leaderboard. Others > Credits.
- Game Multi > Local (same Wi-Fi; asks for Local Network access; Bonjour service _DodgeServer, the game's 2009 code name) or Online (Game Center; invitations work too) > 2 / 3 / 4 Players > act; the host's act is played, and a bullet colour already taken moves to a free one. Difficulty is Normal. Lives are shared (3 per player), the score is a team score; a fallen ship returns at the next act, and an empty pool gets one life there. Enemies are tougher with more ships; act Rain adds a ring of four enemies every 18 s for three or four players. A match needs the other devices; one device reaches the waiting screen. The first iPhone screenshot was taken during a three-player match.

GAME CENTER is offered at launch. If it is declined, Scores, and Online once players and act are chosen, show where to sign in (iOS Settings > Game Center).

No purchases, ads, user-generated content, chat, web views or analytics. Network traffic: Game Center, and game data between the players' devices during a match.
